2. What the app is for

MigraineDiary helps people privately record migraine episodes, symptoms, possible triggers, medications, medication intake, appointments, and related notes; view summaries; and create reports for use with healthcare professionals. It is a self-tracking tool and does not provide a diagnosis or replace medical advice.

3. Health information stays on your device

The migraine, symptom, trigger, medication, appointment and diary information you enter is stored locally on your device using Apple data-storage technologies. The current app design does not require an account or send your health diary to a server operated by the developer. The developer cannot access or recover entries stored only on your device. Your device operating system may back up app data according to your Apple and device backup settings.

4. Optional weather and location information

If you use the weather feature, the app requests weather data from Open-Meteo. A weather request includes the coordinates of the selected place and the relevant date range. Searching for a city sends your search text to the Open-Meteo geocoding service. If you tap “Use current location” and allow location access, iOS provides location coordinates; the app rounds latitude and longitude to two decimal places before storing or using them for weather requests. Existing selected locations may cause weather updates when you open relevant diary views. Migraine entries, medication records and personal health notes are not sent to Open-Meteo for weather requests. Open-Meteo can receive technical connection information, including IP addresses, and states its troubleshooting logs, which may include geographic coordinates, are deleted after 90 days. See: https://open-meteo.com/en/terms . Weather data credit: Open-Meteo, under CC BY 4.0.

5. Permissions and notifications

The app asks for location permission only to find the weather for your current area when you choose that option. You can instead select a city manually. Location access can be changed or revoked in iOS Settings. If you enable medication reminders, the app uses on-device notifications; you can disable notification permissions in iOS Settings. The app does not require location permission to record migraines.

6. Exports and backups

You may choose to generate a PDF medical report or export a JSON backup. These files can contain sensitive health details, medication histories, appointment information, and notes. The JSON export is not encrypted by the app. When you choose where to save, upload or share a file, the destination service or recipient may process it under their own privacy terms. Keep exported files in a secure place and share them only with people you trust.

7. Advertising, analytics and third parties

The release design described in this policy does not include targeted advertising, third-party advertising networks, or developer-operated analytics that upload your health diary. Open-Meteo is an external provider used for the optional weather service. Any new external services or SDKs must be reviewed, and this policy updated, before they are added. Apple may process technical information independently in connection with App Store distribution, device services, and user-controlled backups.

8. Data retention and deletion

Local records remain on the device until you remove them using available app functions or remove the app and its local data. Removing the app does not automatically remove copies that you exported, shared or placed in backups. The developer has no central user account or copy of your local diary to delete. If a feature is added that uploads data to developer-managed servers, the deletion process and this notice will be updated.

9. Your choices and rights

You control the entries you create, can decide whether to use the optional weather feature, and can manage location and notification permissions in iOS Settings. If you are in the European Economic Area, you may also have the right to lodge a complaint with your competent data-protection authority. Some requests cannot be fulfilled by the developer for data stored exclusively on your device because the developer cannot access that data.
